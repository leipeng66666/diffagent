Silicalite-1 (MFI) exhibits the highest performance for water removal in natural gas purification based on experimental diffusion coefficients, as it shows a self-diffusivity of 4.5×10⁻⁸ m²/s at 298 K from PFG NMR (doi:10.1016/j.mri.2012.02.026). This value is orders of magnitude larger than those of other zeolites measured by the same technique, such as natural chabazite which reaches only 5.9×10⁻¹² m²/s (doi:10.1016/S1387-1811(98)00083-3). The comparison is restricted to experimental PFG NMR data to avoid cross-method bias, and the high diffusivity suggests rapid water transport kinetics.
